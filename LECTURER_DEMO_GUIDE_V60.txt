ONE-SENTENCE POSITIONING
JurisTwin gives management one trusted answer from approved enterprise sources, protects private/client data, updates from current governed evidence, requires human publication authority and keeps every sensitive action traceable.

KEY Q&A
Q: Do you train the AI on private emails/Teams chats?
A: No. Enterprise evidence is scoped for runtime retrieval. Client evidence is never used as model-training data. Personal Teams DMs are excluded.

Q: What if most people in Teams say something different from the Product Owner?
A: The official approved source wins. Majority is only a fallback among equally authoritative, approved-scope sources.

Q: Can a manager export all customer data?
A: Not by default. The server enforces role-based export rights; the manager demo role can export a masked report, while full export is reserved for authorised Compliance roles.

Q: Is the demo static?
A: No. Every question recomputes from current governed state. Source-scope changes affect subsequent retrieval immediately, and signed incoming evidence can be received live while remaining quarantined until approved.

Q: Why can’t Publish be clicked sometimes?
A: The Governance Gate deliberately blocks publication when an assurance check fails. v6.0 also self-checks the seeded operational-case cohort so stale demo data cannot falsely produce a zero-case mismatch.
