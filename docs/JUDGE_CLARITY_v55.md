# JurisTwin Sentinel v5.5 — Judge Clarity Layer

## Purpose
The finals UI now follows a strict communication rule: **plain-English answer first, technical proof second**.

## Conflict Map
Every seeded conflict includes:
1. the exact approved evidence message;
2. the exact conflicting message;
3. all additional conflicting sources;
4. a plain-English explanation of why the messages are incompatible;
5. why the approved source remains canonical;
6. the customer consequence;
7. the existing graph, authority, version and blast-radius proof underneath.

For the flagship income-document conflict the UI explicitly shows the Product Owner approval allowing bank statements against the stale payslip-only FSD/frontline instructions.

## Judge Challenge
The result panel puts the judge's exact unseen text beside the highest-authority approved source. It explains:
- why they conflict;
- which source wins and why;
- the customer blast radius;
- quarantine status.

Policy atoms and the learned/symbolic AI model remain available as technical drill-down controls.

## Digital Twin
The recommendation panel now answers:
- **Why not A?**
- **Why not B?**
- **Why C?**

The flagship non-technical takeaway is: **Option C fixes the organisation, not just one document.**

It also shows the before → after customer outcomes for delay, complaint exposure, cases still exposed and policy alignment. Monte Carlo, sensitivity, Pareto and model-boundary details remain behind **See technical proof**.

## Design principle
A judge should understand the problem and recommendation without knowing what policy atoms, BFS, Monte Carlo or Pareto analysis mean. Those mechanisms are preserved as proof when technical depth is requested.
