# JurisTwin Sentinel v5.3 — Finals Demonstration Flow

## The six-stage operating model

**CONNECT → EXPOSE → SIMULATE → RECOMMEND → APPROVE → PROTECT**

### 1. CONNECT — Secure Enterprise Memory
Open **Final Flow → Connect** or **Platform → Secure Enterprise Memory**.
Show the source chain: Teams / Outlook / Gmail / Documents / Customer Data → AI Classification → Vector Evidence Vault.
Switch **Manager → Officer → Intern** to prove role-aware retrieval and redaction.

### 2. EXPOSE — Living Decision Digital Twin
Open the critical conflict.
Show Outlook approved evidence, Teams informal guidance, outdated FSD/training rules and customer impact in one network.
Drag nodes, inspect source/authority/version and open the blast-radius proof.

### 3. SIMULATE — White-Box Future Simulator
Run the 1,500-scenario stress test.
Compare:
- A — Take No Action
- B — Update the FSD Only
- C — Align the Complete Process

### 4. RECOMMEND — Robust Decision Certificate
Show why Option C remains strongest under uncertainty/sensitivity testing and why it fixes both the document conflict and downstream operational gap.

### 5. APPROVE — Governance Gate
Send Option C to governance.
Show Authority / Impact / Simulation / Ledger / Security gate checks.
Approve and publish Decision JT-084.

Expected operating propagation:
- 27 applications affected/protected
- 1 rejected case flagged
- 8 QA tests updated
- 3 documents superseded across the operating model
- 4 officers notified

The JT-084 contract itself tracks two direct policy documents; the third superseded document is downstream training guidance in the broader propagation result.

### 6. PROTECT — AI Bodyguard + Decision Ledger
Open Decision Ledger and show JT-084 version history.
Then open AI Bodyguard and simulate the QA-014 Credit Policy v4.2 modification.
Demonstrate Review Activity, Revoke Access, Explain, Escalate, Authorise Overwrite, and Restore Version.
