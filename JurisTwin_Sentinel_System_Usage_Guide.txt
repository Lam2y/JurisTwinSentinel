1. WHAT JURISTWIN SENTINEL DOES

JurisTwin Sentinel is a decision-control system for companies.

Simple flow:

DETECT → EXPLAIN → SIMULATE → APPROVE → PROPAGATE → AUDIT → PROTECT

It finds conflicting information, explains the conflict, compares possible fixes, lets a human approve the best decision, updates the process, records the decision, and protects it from unauthorised changes later.

17. SIMPLE EXPLANATION FOR JUDGES

“JurisTwin first finds where the organisation disagrees, then shows the evidence, simulates the consequences of each resolution, lets humans approve the best decision, propagates that decision across the process, records it in an auditable ledger, and protects it from being silently changed later.”


18. WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT FROM A NORMAL CHATBOT

A normal chatbot mainly answers questions.

A normal RAG system mainly retrieves documents and generates answers from them.

JurisTwin goes further:

1. Detects contradictions between enterprise sources.
2. Connects evidence into a decision context.
3. Shows which information is outdated or conflicting.
4. Simulates possible resolutions.
5. Keeps a human in the approval loop.
6. Creates a formal Decision Contract.
7. Propagates the approved decision.
8. Records the decision in a tamper-evident ledger.
9. Monitors later activity that could break the approved decision.
10. Restores the trusted version when needed.
