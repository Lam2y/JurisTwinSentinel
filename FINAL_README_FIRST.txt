TRUTH BOUNDARIES — DO NOT OVERCLAIM
- Outlook/Teams/SharePoint branded connectors are deterministic finals adapters unless a real tenant is configured.
- The genuine machine-to-machine ingress proof is the HMAC-SHA256 signed webhook.
- Retrieval is local BM25 + cosine. ChromaDB is a pilot target, not the runtime answer path.
- The policy AI is local TF-IDF + Logistic Regression with a deterministic Policy Atom verifier.
- The Decision Twin is a white-box, prototype-calibrated simulator stress-tested across 1,500 scenarios; do not call it trained forecasting ML.
- Client evidence is used for authorised retrieval/impact analysis only. It is not added to the classifier training corpus.
- The decision ledger is append-only SHA-256-linked with HMAC proof verification; do not call it blockchain.

DO NOT DEMO
- fake vendor Connect/Sync as though it contacted a live Microsoft/Google tenant

- terminal debugging for more than ~15 seconds
